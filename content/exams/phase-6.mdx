## What this covers

This exam closes phase 6. Every node in the phase is examined:

- **6.1 Serving a model** - latency percentiles and input validation
- **6.2 Containerize and deploy** - layer caching and multi-stage image size

## How to prepare

The exam is timed and open-tool: Python, a notebook or a calculator are all allowed, and you are expected to use them. There is nothing to memorise. If you can compute a percentile, a PSI and a KS statistic from a small table, trace which Docker layers rebuild, and apply a gate by hand, you are ready. Read each prompt for the rounding and unit it asks for. The numbers change on a retake, so rework the method, not the answers.
