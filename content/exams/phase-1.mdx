## What this covers

This exam closes Phase 1. It has ten questions, two for each node:

- **1.1 Linear algebra intuition** - multiplying matrices of different shapes, determinants, and eigenvalues.
- **1.2 Derivatives and the chain rule** - a hand-derived chain rule through a sigmoid, and a finite-difference estimate.
- **1.3 Probability and distributions** - exact binomial probabilities, and Poisson counts for a rate and a window.
- **1.4 Statistical inference** - how false positives pile up across many tests, and a confidence interval from a standard error.
- **1.5 Gradient descent by hand** - stepping the update rule yourself, and finding the learning rate where it stops converging.

Every answer is a number you compute and type in.

## How to prepare

The exam is open-tool and timed: use Python, a notebook, a calculator and your own session code, including your gradient checker. There is no need to memorise formulas, but be able to rebuild each one from what it means. Read each prompt for the exact form it wants: decimal places, a fraction rather than a percentage, which bound. The questions change on a retake, so remembering answers will not help; redoing the session you stumbled on will.
