## What this covers

One exam for the whole of phase 3. Every node has two questions:

- **3.1 Feature engineering** - a ratio feature and a window count, both built strictly before the cutoff
- **3.2 Encoding and scaling** - how wide an encoded matrix is, and what a train-fitted scaler does to an unseen value
- **3.3 Clustering** - one k-means assign-and-update step, and the silhouette of a single point
- **3.4 Dimensionality reduction** - explained variance from eigenvalues, and projecting a new point with PCA
- **3.5 Anomaly detection** - what a contamination cut flags, and what a z-score rule misses

## How to prepare

This is an open-tool exam. Use Python, a notebook or a calculator, and keep your node notes open: nothing here needs memorising. Every answer is a number you compute and type in. Two questions use committed datasets, so have pandas ready. Read each prompt for its conventions: whether a boundary is inclusive, how many decimal places, fraction or percent. Enter just the number, with no units or percent sign. The numbers change on a retake, so learn the method rather than the answers.
