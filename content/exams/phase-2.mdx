## What this covers

One exam for the whole of phase 2. Every node has at least one question:

- **2.1 Framing an ML problem** - population, label and cutoff on the retail data
- **2.2 Linear and logistic regression** - a prediction from given coefficients, and reading a coefficient as an odds multiplier
- **2.3 Validation and leakage** - train-only scaling and out-of-fold target encoding
- **2.4 Metrics that match the problem** - F1 at a threshold, and choosing a threshold from a cost
- **2.5 Trees and forests** - Gini impurity and the gain of a split
- **2.6 Gradient boosting** - residuals, a leaf value and the learning rate
- **2.7 Model diagnosis** - turning an error category into an upper bound

## How to prepare

This is an open-tool exam. Use Python, a notebook or a calculator, and keep your node notes open: nothing here needs memorising. Every answer is a number you compute and type in. One question uses a committed dataset, so have pandas ready. Read each prompt for its conventions: whether a boundary is inclusive, how many decimal places, fraction or percent. Enter just the number, with no units or percent sign. The numbers change on a retake, so learn the method rather than the answers.
